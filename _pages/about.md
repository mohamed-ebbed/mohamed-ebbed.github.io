I am a PhD Student in the [Geometry in Machine Learning Group](https://geometryinml.cs.uni-bonn.de) at the University of Bonn and [Lamarr Institute for Machine Learning and Artificial Intelligence](https://lamarr-institute.org/de/) supervised by [Prof. Dr. Zorah Lähner](https://geometryinml.cs.uni-bonn.de/team/zorah/). Previously, I have obtained my M.Sc degree from the Technical University of Munich.

### Research

I'm interested in 4D Reconstruction, Geometry Processing, Shape Analysis and Inverse Rendering.
